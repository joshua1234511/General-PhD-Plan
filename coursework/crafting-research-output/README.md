# Crafting Research Output

**Course Work 1 · 3 credits**
**Instructor(s):** Prof. Mahadeo Jaiswal / Prof. Sandip Mukhopadhyay
[← Course Work 1 index](../README.md)

## Overview
**Format:** sessions are **student-led** — scholars present assigned articles from
their own **Word documents** (instructor discourages PowerPoint) and the class
discusses multiple perspectives.

## Assessments
<!-- Weightage: assignments, presentations, term paper, end-term -->
- [ ] Per-reading **Word document**: summary **+** reflection (what you like,
  relevance, limitations, positives), ready to present in class.

## Key readings

> **Provisional** — suggested canonical readings for a PhD "Crafting Research
> Output" course. Replace with the official syllabus list once received; store
> PDFs in [reading-materials/](reading-materials/).

**Books (craft & style)**
- Booth, Colomb, Williams, Bizup & FitzGerald — *The Craft of Research*
- Wendy Laura Belcher — *Writing Your Journal Article in Twelve Weeks*
- Howard S. Becker — *Writing for Social Scientists*
- Helen Sword — *Stylish Academic Writing*
- William Zinsser — *On Writing Well*

**Articles (publishing in management/IS)**
- Whetten (1989), *AMR* — "What Constitutes a Theoretical Contribution?"
- Webster & Watson (2002), *MIS Quarterly* — "Analyzing the Past to Prepare for the Future: Writing a Literature Review"
- Bem (2003) — "Writing the Empirical Journal Article"
- Grant & Pollock (2011), *AMJ* editorial — publishing tips (titles, abstracts, framing)
- Sparrowe & Mayer (2011), *AMJ* — "Publishing in AMJ, Part 4: Grounding hypotheses"
- Pratt (2009), *AMJ* — "For the lack of a boilerplate: Tips on writing up qualitative research"
- Ragins (2012), *AMR* — "Reflections on the craft of clear writing"
