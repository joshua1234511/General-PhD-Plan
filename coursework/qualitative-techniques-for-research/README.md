## Overview
Qualitative research design following the interpretive school — used to strongly
theorise concepts or build new theory in social-science / organisational studies.
A step-by-step guide plus traditional and contemporary tools & techniques for
qualitative inquiry: what it is, why/when to use it, how to theorise, conduct,
and report it, and the critical issues for publishing qualitative work.

## Objectives
Conceptual + practical knowledge of qualitative research inquiry: what it is, why
it's needed, when to use it, how to theorise/conduct/report it, and what it takes
to publish qualitative papers.

**Competency Goal CG3 (Research), LO 3.1:** create a clear research plan, apply
correct data analysis, draw well-supported conclusions via critical thinking.

## Sessions & readings

**Module I — Qualitative Research Design in Mgmt & Org Studies**
- **S1–2** Course intro · organizational research · research paradigms · theorizing
  - Gioia & Pitre (1990), *AMR* 15(4): 584–602 — *Multiparadigm Perspectives on Theory Building*
- **S3–4** Qualitative research in mgmt/org studies · rigor
  - Plakoyiannaki et al. (2019), *MOR* 15(2): 217–234 — Rethinking Qualitative Scholarship in Emerging Markets
  - Köhler et al. (2022), *ORM* 25(2): 183–210 — Templates in qualitative research methods
  - Tracy (2010), *Qual. Inquiry* 16(10): 837–851 — Eight "Big-Tent" criteria
- **S5** Innovation in qualitative methods
  - Lê & Schmid (2022), *ORM* 25(2): 308–336 — The practice of innovating research methods
  - Bansal et al. (2018), *AMJ* 61(4): 1189–1195 — New ways of seeing through qualitative research

**Module II — Qualitative Models, Data Sources & Collection**
- **S6–8** Sampling & generalization · data collection · triangulation · interviews · focus groups · transcribing
  - Ivanova-Gongne et al. (2018), *Ind. Marketing Mgmt* 70: 193–204
- **S9–12** Models: Case study, Grounded Theory, Ethnography, Phenomenology · data collection across models
  - Eisenhardt & Graebner (2007), *AMJ* 50(1): 25–32 — Theory building from cases
  - Wiesche et al. (2017), *MISQ* 41(3): 685–701 — Grounded theory in IS research
  - Cunliffe (2010), *ORM* 13(2): 224–239 — Retelling Tales of the Field (ethnography)
  - Gill (2014), *ORM* 17(2): 118–137 — Phenomenology for organizational research

**Module III — Analysis, Theorizing & Reporting**
- **S13–16** Analytical strategies · content & thematic analysis · QCA · software
  - Lester et al. (2020), *HRD Review* 19(1): 94–106 — Learning to Do Qualitative Data Analysis
  - Thomann & Maggetti (2020), *Soc. Methods & Research* 49(2): 356–386 — QCA
- **S17** Quality of analysis · ethics & reporting
  - Jonsen et al. (2018), *ORM* 21(1): 30–67 — Convincing Qualitative Research: Persuasive Writing

**Module IV — Term Project: Crafting a Qualitative Research Paper**
- **S18–19** Research-proposal formulation (qualitative design) · in/out-of-class
- **S20** Term-project presentation (CG3/LO3.1)
