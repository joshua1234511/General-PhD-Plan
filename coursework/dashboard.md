## 1 · Qualitative Techniques for Research (QLTR)
**Covers:** research **paradigms** (interpretive/functionalist etc.); qualitative
methodologies (**Grounded Theory / phenomenology / ethnography**); sampling,
**triangulation**, **constant comparison**; the **Gioia** data structure (1st-order →
2nd-order → aggregate); rigor, analysis and reporting.
→ [README](qualitative-techniques-for-research/README.md) · [briefing-notes](qualitative-techniques-for-research/reading-materials/briefing-notes.md)
**Assessment:** Participation **20%** · Critical analysis of a paper (pairs) **30%** · Term project + presentation **50%**.

## 2 · Quantitative Techniques for Research (QNTR)
**Starts:** QNTR-1/2 on **02 Aug**. Runs Sundays, mostly through Oct (ends ~04 Oct).
**Prep:** set up **SPSS / R / SmartPLS** before sessions ramp; get the syllabus +
assessment breakdown (README is a stub until the outline lands).
→ [README](quantitative-techniques-for-research/README.md) · [notes.md](quantitative-techniques-for-research/notes.md)
**Assessment:** TBD (syllabus awaited).

## 3 · Management Teaching Method (MTM)
**Covers:** *Win As Much As You Can* simulation (experiential cycle; cooperation vs
competition); **non-cognitive skills** (Gabrieli et al.); *Great Teachers Don't Teach*
(constructivism); **facilitator vs instructor**; **active listening & empathy**;
**feedback** as two-way (specificity/empathy/inquiry; Wiggins' seven keys).
→ [README](management-teaching-method/README.md) · [briefing-notes](management-teaching-method/reading-materials/briefing-notes.md)
**Assessment:** Storytelling **20%** · Feedback project **20%** · Practice teaching **30%** · Critical reflection **15%** · Participation **15%**. *(Video-on mandatory.)*

## 4 · Crafting Research Output (CRO)
**Format:** student-led — present **from a Word doc** (no PPT). Per-reading summary +
reflection on assigned articles; the class discusses multiple perspectives.
→ [README](crafting-research-output/README.md) · [briefing-notes](crafting-research-output/reading-materials/briefing-notes.md)
**Assessment:** per-reading Word **summary + reflection** (full weightage TBC).
