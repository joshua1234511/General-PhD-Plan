## 5. Award of degree — publication requirements ⚠️

Many programmes require published (or accepted) journal papers before thesis submission.
Common elements:

- A **minimum number or combination** of papers, often by journal-list rating
  (e.g. ABDC, ABS/AJG, FT50) or indexing (Scopus, Web of Science).
- **First authorship** by the scholar.
- **Institutional affiliation** on the counted papers.
- Rules on whether **conference papers** or **published cases** count.

**ABDC ⇄ ABS (AJG) rough equivalence** (commonly used; check whether your programme
accepts it):

| ABDC | ABS |
|:----:|:---:|
| A* | 4* and 4 |
| A | 3 |
| B | 2 |
| C | 1 |

> **Planning implication:** journal review cycles are long. If your programme has a
> publication gate, start a pipeline aimed at it from the second year, and confirm the exact
> rule with your doctoral office in writing — informal summaries often differ from the manual.
