## 1. Governance & key bodies

| Body | Composition | Role |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Doctoral Committee** | Constituted by Director; Director nominates a Chairperson | Decides all operating issues of the doctoral programme; major policy → Academic Council |
| **Doctoral Office** | — | Facilitates all PhD/PhD-WP activities (seminars, submissions, forms) |
| **Comprehensive Exam Committee (CEC)** | Chair from scholar's area of specialization + one member from a related area + one from Research Methodology (if area has one faculty, take the other two from related areas) | Conducts the comprehensive examination |
| **Thesis Advisory Committee (TAC)** | Chief Thesis Supervisor (TAC Chair) + 1 internal faculty from another area + 1 from a reputed overseas institute/university | Guides the thesis work |
| **Thesis Examination Committee (TEC)** | TAC Chair + 1 TAC member + 2 external experts (1 national, 1 international), appointed with Director's approval | Examines the final thesis |

**Overseas TAC member requirement:** must have worked in the given area, with a
minimum of **2 years' experience** at a reputed foreign university and a **good
publication record**.

## 2. Coursework (Total: 33 credits)

Coursework spans **Terms IV–VI** (manual: "Course Work for Second Year"). The
**Course of Independent Study (CIS)** is undertaken in the second year of the
programme, **commencing from Term V**.

| Term | Components | Credits |
|------|-----------|:------:|
| **Term IV** | Quantitative Techniques for Research | 3 |
| | Qualitative Techniques for Research | 3 |
| | Management Teaching Method | 3 |
| | Crafting Research Output (CRO) | 3 |
| | PGP / other-area Elective Courses | 6 |
| **Term V** | Case Writing (CW) *(may extend to Term VI)* | 3 |
| | Domain-Specific Core / Area Elective Courses | 9 |
| | **CIS (Course of Independent Study)** *(may extend to Term Break)* | 3 |
| **Term VI** | Elective Courses | — |

> **CIS:** 3-credit independent study; a credit requirement, not optional.
> **Case Writing:** 3 credits; cases published in **Ivey/Harvard** count as a
> "C"-category publication toward the degree (see §5).

## 3. Grading & progression gates

A maximum of **one F overall** across first- and second-year coursework is allowed,
and that **F must not be in the area of affiliation**.

**First year — must NOT accumulate:**
- a D in any course if an F was obtained in one non-area course; **or**
- a D in more than two courses if no F was obtained in any first-year course.
- At most **one D** allowed in area-specific courses.

**Second year — at no stage should the scholar accumulate:**
- a D in any course if an F was obtained in one non-area course; **or**
- a D in more than **one** course if no F was obtained in any second-year course.
- **No D** allowed in area-specific courses.
- **No F** allowed if one was already obtained in a first-year course.

**Minimum standards to proceed to the comprehensive exam:**
- **CGPA ≥ 5.50** at the end of the first year **and** the second year of coursework.
- **GPA ≥ 6.00** in Area-Specific Doctoral Courses at the end of the second year.

> One F or one D in any course is treated as a **full** F/D irrespective of the
> course's credit weight.

### Attendance penalties (PhD-WP)

| Attendance | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| ≥ 50% and < 60% | One grade drop (e.g. A+ → A) |
| ≥ 40% and < 50% | Two grade drops (A+ → A−) |
| ≥ 30% and < 40% | Three grade drops (A+ → B+) |
| < 30% | **F grade awarded** |

*(Regular PhD thresholds are stricter: bands run 60/70/80/90%; < 60% = F.)*

## 4. Milestones & statutory timelines

| # | Milestone | Timeline (from the manual) |
|---|-----------|----------------------------|
| 1 | **Comprehensive Exam (CE)** | Within **3 months** of completing coursework + CIS (with required grades). Deferral up to **6 months** total, with Doctoral Committee/PhD Chair permission. |
| 2 | **Identify Thesis Advisor** | Communicate to PhD Office in writing (with faculty consent) after passing CE. |
| 3 | **Propose TAC** | Within **6 weeks** of passing the comprehensive exam. |
| 4 | **Thesis Proposal + open seminar** | Within **6 months** of TAC formation (extension up to **3 months** with PhD-committee + TAC recommendation). |
| 5 | **Quarterly Progress Report** | Every quarter from date of TAC formation, in the prescribed format (**Annexure-15**), signed by TAC. Email approval from external TAC members is acceptable. |
| 6 | **Thesis Seminar / Internal Defense** | Presented only **after** submitting the approved draft thesis to the Doctoral Office; feedback must be incorporated into the final thesis. |
| 7 | **Final Draft Thesis Submission** | Within **1 month** of the thesis seminar. Include a detailed abstract **≤ 10 pages**, soft + hard copies, and TAC-signed approval forms. |
| 8 | **TEC examination** | Examiners give feedback within **45–60 days** of receiving the draft (Annexure-13). Verdict: accept / minor / major modifications. |
| 9 | **Major modifications** | Resubmit as a "Revised Draft" (TAC-approved) within **6 months** of competent-authority approval; first-draft evaluation repeats. |
| 10 | **Final Thesis Defense** | Open presentation; scholar **must be on campus**. Domestic TEC members attend in person; international members may attend virtually. |

**TEC attendance:** domestic members attend pre-submission/defense **in person**;
international members **may attend virtually** if required.

## 5. Award of degree — publication requirements ⚠️

**This is the hard publication gate — it supersedes the informal "3–5 papers" phrasing.**

At the time of **internal thesis submission**, the scholar must have **published**
(or hold a **letter of acceptance**) at least **one** of the following:

- **(1 B and 1 C)** ABDC-rated papers; **or**
- **(1 A)** ABDC-rated paper; **or**
- **4 Scopus-indexed** + **(1 C)** ABDC-rated paper; **or**
- **2 Scopus-indexed** + **(1 B)** ABDC-rated paper; **or**
- **6 Scopus-indexed** papers.

**Rules that qualify a publication:**
- The scholar must be the **first author**.
- Journals must **also** be indexed in **Scopus or Web of Science** — a journal in
  the ABDC list but NOT in Scopus/WoS does **not** count.
- Cases published in **Ivey/Harvard** = equivalent to a **"C"** category publication.

**ABDC ⇄ ABS equivalence** (if publishing in ABS-listed journals):

| ABDC | ABS |
|:----:|:---:|
| A* | 4* and 4 |
| A | 3 |
| B | 2 |
| C | 1 |

**Other conditions for the award of title:**
- Successfully defended thesis, accepted for the award of title.
- Deposit **Convocation and Alumni fee** (amount per Institute decision).
- Submit required soft copies (pen drive) / hard copies of the final approved thesis.
- **No financial liability or dues** to the Institute.
- Return the **original ID card** issued by the Institute.

> **Planning implication:** conference / Scopus-proceedings papers alone do **not**
> satisfy the gate. A publication pipeline **must** land at least the equivalent of
> one **ABDC-A** or one **ABDC-B + one ABDC-C** journal publication (first-author,
> Scopus/WoS-indexed) **before** internal thesis submission in Year 4. Given journal
> review timelines, target this from Year 2 onward.
